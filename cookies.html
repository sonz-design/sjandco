Cookie policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains how sjeandco.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Our own cookies

We don't use cookies of our own, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Third-party services

Some features of this site are provided by other companies, which may set cookies or collect technical information (such as your IP address) when you use them:

  • Google Forms, which powers our enquiry and application forms. Google may set cookies when a form opens.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the fonts on this site. Your browser connects to Google to load them.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website and may log basic technical information for security.

These companies are responsible for their own cookies. You can read more in Google's cookie policy.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block third-party cookies, our forms may not load. You can always contact us by email at enquiry@sjeandco.com instead.

Changes

If we add any cookies in future, such as analytics, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.
